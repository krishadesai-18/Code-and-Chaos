📊 Concept Map

Predicting Stock Market Crash with Bayesian Generalised Pareto Regression

Bayesian GPR for Stock Market Crash Prediction
🎯 Research Problem
Extreme Market Events
Stock market crashes are rare but cause massive financial disruption and wealth erosion
Limitation of Traditional Models
Standard GPD models assume constant parameters, ignoring current market conditions
❌ No covariate integration
❌ Static risk assessment
❌ Cannot adapt to volatility regimes
Real-World Impact
5% single-day drops trigger margin calls, forced liquidations, and market instability
🔬 Methodology
GPD Regression (GPR)
Scale parameter as function of covariates: log(σᵢ) = Xᵢ'β
✓ Shape parameter (ξ): constant across observations
✓ Scale parameter (σ): varies with market conditions
✓ Threshold (μ): 2% daily loss
Bayesian Framework
Four prior distributions tested for regularization
⭐ Cauchy: Heavy-tailed, flexible (WINNER)
Lasso: Sparsity-inducing (worst performance)
Ridge: Smooth shrinkage (moderate)
Zellner's g-prior: Data-informed (slow, 2477×)
MAP Estimation
Maximum a posteriori for efficient model exploration using BFGS optimization
📈 Data & Features
Dataset Characteristics
NSE Nifty 50 Index: Sept 2007 - June 2025 (17+ years)
Covers 2008 financial crisis
Covers COVID-19 pandemic crash
330 extreme events (losses > 2%)
Volatility Measures (Covariates)
Three sources capturing domestic and global risk
🇮🇳 Nifty 50: Empirical & Garman-Klass volatility
🇺🇸 S&P 500: Global market spillover
🥇 Gold: Flight-to-safety indicator
Volatility Calculation Methods
Two complementary approaches
EWMA: σ²ₜ = α·s²ₜ₋₁ + (1-α)·r²ₜ
Garman-Klass: Uses High/Low/Open/Close prices
Correlation: 0.71 in log scale
📊 Key Results
Model Performance (Cauchy Prior)
Best across all metrics
RMSE: 1.58 (lowest)
AIC: 298.06 (lowest)
BIC: 322.20 (lowest)
Nonlinear Volatility-Crash Relationship
Risk increases dramatically with volatility
Low volatility (10th percentile): 5% crash probability
High volatility (90th percentile): 65% crash probability
13× increase in crash risk!
Expected Loss Predictions
Conditional on exceeding 2% threshold
Low volatility: ~2.4% expected loss
High volatility: ~7%+ expected loss
Global Market Integration
International factors significantly predict Indian crashes
S&P 500 volatility: Strong spillover effect
Gold volatility: Flight-to-safety signal
Markets are interconnected, not isolated
